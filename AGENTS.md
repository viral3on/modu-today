# Editorial publishing requirements

When the owner requests an article, include a relevant representative thumbnail as part of the work. This is a standing owner preference.

- Every published reading article must have a nonempty `image` URL and meaningful Korean `imageAlt`.
- Create or select a topic-specific image with appropriate usage rights. Store generated project assets in `assets/editorial/` or the established image storage, not only in a temporary directory.
- Do not replace existing covers unless asked. Do not use the text-only card as a completed thumbnail.
- Keep drafts and previews usable before a cover is ready. Publication and static builds enforce covers for published reading articles; product guides are separate.
- Verify the actual image loads in the reading list, homepage card where applicable, and article page. Include the image when checking social-sharing metadata.
- Vary the visual style for each new article thumbnail to suit its subject (for example photography, editorial collage, flat illustration, watercolor, 3D rendering, or a clean infographic). This is a standing owner preference. Do not reuse one illustration treatment, color palette, or composition across a batch; changing only the depicted objects is not sufficient. Review neighboring article covers for visual variety while preserving readability at card size.
